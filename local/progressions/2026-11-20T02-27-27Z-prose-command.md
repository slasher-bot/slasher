## What happened

- `/prose` reviews content through the subcommands `edit`, `suggest`, `critique`, `accept`, and `reject`, in the PROSE command domain, which replaces REVIEW. `/prose help` prints its help.
- `/edit`, `/review`, `/accept`, and `/reject` are short forms of `/prose edit`, `/prose suggest`, `/prose accept`, and `/prose reject`. `/review accept` and `/review reject` still work.
- `/prose edit` numbers the sentences of each paragraph s1, s2, and `/prose edit p4 s2` goes to one. Each finding carries a numbered suggestion, put through the selection interface as *Accept*, *Reject*, or *Ignore*. Accept writes the change at once. Ignore leaves the suggestion pending under its number.
- Suggestions from `/prose edit` and `/prose suggest` share one numbering, so `/accept 3` names one suggestion.
- `/prose critique` judges the content whole, and gives a numbered list of findings, most important first. It changes nothing.
- `/print-and-review` and `/pr` are removed, with their rule, logged as suspended.
- The review rule is renamed the prose-suggest-suggests-numbered-edits-and-accept-or-reject-settles-them rule. No rule has a compressed text to check.
- No loop record: the work is rules, skills, and help, which is content work in this project.
- The automated suite passes: 121 tests.
