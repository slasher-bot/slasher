# An improvement is a kind of item

- **State:** Realized
- **Tags:** [idea]

**Items would gain a kind, the improvement, beside the idea, the plan, the design, the observation, the aspiration, and the work item.** An improvement would record a change to something that already exists, as distinct from an idea for something new.

**Not yet settled:** how an improvement differs from an idea and from a work item, which states it takes, its command, and whether records already written as ideas would move to it.

## Settled

- **2026-11-07** — An improvement is a proposed change to something that already exists. It is not yet decided, like an idea, and concludes Realized when the change exists.
- **2026-11-07** — Ideas already written keep their `[idea]` tag. Only new items take `[improvement]`.
- **2026-11-07** — `/improvement` creates an item tagged `[improvement]`, as an alias of `/item improvement`.
