# Observation items become note items

- **State:** Realized
- **Tags:** [idea]

**Items now tagged `[observation]` become note items, and the word *observation* is kept for Design By Efferent alone.** In Design By Efferent, an observation is what a test reads about the effect of an actuation.

**As the engineer stated it:** "Observation" items become "note" items. Observeration stays exclusive to DBE
