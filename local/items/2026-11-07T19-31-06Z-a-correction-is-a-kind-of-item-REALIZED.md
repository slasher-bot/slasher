# A correction is a kind of item

- **State:** Realized
- **Tags:** [idea]

**Items would gain a kind, the correction.**

**Not yet settled:** what a correction records, and how it differs from a mistake, a problem, and an improvement. Which states it takes. Whether it gets its own command.

## Settled

- **2026-11-07** — A correction is a proposed change that puts right something that exists and is wrong, such as a false statement in a rule. It concludes Realized when the fix exists. An improvement is a change to something that is not wrong.
- **2026-11-07** — A correction takes the states every item takes.
- **2026-11-07** — `/correction` creates an item tagged `[correction]`, as an alias of `/item correction`.

## History

As the engineer first stated it: "Add \"correction\" as an item type"
