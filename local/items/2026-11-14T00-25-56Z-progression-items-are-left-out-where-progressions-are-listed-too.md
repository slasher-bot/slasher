# Progression items are left out where progressions are listed too

- **State:** Completed
- **Tags:** [correction] [work] [rework]

When current items and progressions are printed together, as is the case with some commands, items in the Progression state should not be printed. When current items are printed on their own, items in the Progression state should be printed.
