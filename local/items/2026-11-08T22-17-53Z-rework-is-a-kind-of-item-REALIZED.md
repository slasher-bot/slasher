# Rework is a kind of item

- **State:** Realized
- **Tags:** [idea]

**Rework is proposed as a kind of item, tagged `[rework]`.** An item of that kind is also tagged `[work]` and `[correction]`.

## Settled

- **2026-11-08** — A rework item is work to redo something already produced that turned out wrong. It carries `[rework]`, `[work]`, and `[correction]`, and concludes as a work item: `Done`, never `Realized`. This is decided by the agent under `/unattended`, in the plan the engineer accepted.

## History

As the engineer first stated it, on 2026-11-08: "Add rework as an item type. Rework is also tagged as [work-item] and [correction]"

On 2026-11-08 the engineer corrected the idea: "Use [work] rather than [work-item]".
