# The command table at startup is abridged

- **State:** Active
- **Tags:** [idea]

**The command table printed at startup is abridged.**

## Settled

- **2026-11-07** — The startup table keeps the full table's first four rows as they are: `/slasher help, /help`, `/?`, `/.`, and `/'`. A comma-separated sentence below it names every other command. `/?` and `/command-table` print the full table. As the engineer stated it: "The table will be much smaller. The table be followed with a comma-separated list in sentence form of the commands removed from the table. These rows remain as-is: the first row with slasher help, the "/?" row, the "/'" row, the "/." row."

- **2026-11-07** — In the startup table, the `/.` and `/'` rows take the descriptions of `/recent` and `/items`, in place of *An alias of /recent* and *An alias of /items*. The full table keeps the alias descriptions. As the engineer stated it: "In the short table, replace the descriptions of aliases, eg: "An alias of /recent" with the actual description"
