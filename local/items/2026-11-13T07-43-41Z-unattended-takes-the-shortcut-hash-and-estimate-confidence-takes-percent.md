# `/unattended` takes the shortcut `/#`, and `/estimate-confidence` takes `/%`

- **State:** Realized
- **Tags:** [idea]

**The shortcut of `/unattended` changes from `/!` to `/#`, and the shortcut of `/estimate-confidence` changes from `/#` to `/%`.**

**As the engineer stated it:** Change "/!" to "/#", and "/#" to "/$"

**As the engineer later stated it:** Actually, "/#" should be "/%", not "/$"

**Progression:** `2026-11-13T07-49-06Z-shortcuts-hash-and-percent`, the feature that builds it.
