# /work-status replaces /status, with the alias /ws

**State:** Open

**Tags:** [work]

**As the engineer stated it:** "/work-status" instead of "/status", with the alias "/ws"

The command that reports the status of the work under way, or of the record named, is `/work-status`, not `/status`. `/ws` is its shortcut alias.
