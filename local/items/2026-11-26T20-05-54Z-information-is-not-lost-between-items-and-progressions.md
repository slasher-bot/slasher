# Information is not lost between an item and its progression

**State:** Open

**Tags:** [work]

**As the engineer stated it:** Make sure that information is not lost between items and progressions. Determine where new information goes.

When a progression is created for an item, the two records name each other, and either can take what is learned as the work goes on: findings, decisions, sources, and the engineer's words. Nothing settles which. Information written to one can be missed by a reader of the other, and the progression's record can end without what the item gathered. This settles where new information goes while a progression carries an item's work, and what each record holds.
