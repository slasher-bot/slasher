# The "No review" option comes first when concluding content

**State:** Open

**As the engineer stated it:** The "no review" option comes first when concluding content progressions

When `/content complete` offers a review before the merge, *No review* is the first option and *Review* the second. Today the content rule names them in the order *Review*, then *No review*.
