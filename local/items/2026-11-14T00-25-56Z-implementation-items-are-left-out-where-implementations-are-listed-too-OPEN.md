# Implementation items are left out where implementations are listed too

- **State:** Open
- **Tags:** [correction] [work] [rework]

When current items and implementations are printed together, as is the case with some commands, items in the Implementation state should not be printed. When current items are printed on their own, items in the Implementation state should be printed.
