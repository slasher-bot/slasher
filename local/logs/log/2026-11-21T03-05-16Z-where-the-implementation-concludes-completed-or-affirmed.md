# Where the implementation concludes Completed or Affirmed, merge implementation/worktree/email-summaries into master, remove its worktree, and delete the branch? — Merge, remove, and delete, selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> Where the implementation concludes Completed or Affirmed, merge implementation/worktree/email-summaries into master, remove its worktree, and delete the branch?

The options:

- **Merge, remove, and delete** — Merge into master with a merge commit, remove the worktree, and delete the branch. It was never pushed.
- **Merge only** — Merge into master, and keep the worktree and the branch
- **Neither** — Leave the branch unmerged, and keep the worktree

The engineer's answer: **Merge, remove, and delete**
