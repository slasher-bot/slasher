# Which states should /resume bring a feature or an experiment back from, to Active? — Paused and Completed, selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> Which states should /resume bring a feature or an experiment back from, to Active?

The options:

- **Paused and Completed** — Suspended, Discontinued, Deferred, and Completed. Mirrors items, which resume from Realized and Done. Resuming a Completed implementation continues its work on a branch.
- **Paused only** — Suspended, Discontinued, and Deferred. A concluded implementation is never resumed. New work on it is a new feature or experiment.
- **Paused and every conclusion** — Suspended, Discontinued, Deferred, Completed, Affirmed, Refuted, and Inconclusive. A verdict can be reopened and reached again.

The engineer's answer: **Paused and Completed**
