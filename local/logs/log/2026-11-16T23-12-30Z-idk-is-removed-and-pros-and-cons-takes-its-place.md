# `/idk` and its rule, idk-answers-the-last-question-with-i-dont-know, are removed, and `/pros-and-cons` takes their place

`/idk` explained the last question, gave the agent's recommendation, and put the question again. `/pros-and-cons` gives each option's pros and cons instead, and `/ask-again` puts the question again. The process help, the help order, and the command-domain rule's list of rows no longer name `/idk`.
