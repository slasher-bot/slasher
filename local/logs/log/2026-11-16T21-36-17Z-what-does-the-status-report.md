# What does the status report? — State, done, and left, selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> What does the status report?

The options:

- **State, done, and left** — The item's state, what of it exists now, and what is left to do. Each in a short list item.
- **State only** — The item's title and its state, in one line.
- **Something else — say what to change** — Some other report. Say what it holds.

The engineer's answer: **State, done, and left**
