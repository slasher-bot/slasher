# Where is the rename worked? — Branch and worktree, selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> Where is the rename worked?

The options:

- **Branch and worktree** — A feature on its own branch in a worktree beside the repository, with the migration record written on master first
- **Branch only** — A feature on its own branch, in this working tree
- **The current branch** — Directly on master, creating no branch

The engineer's answer: **Branch and worktree**
