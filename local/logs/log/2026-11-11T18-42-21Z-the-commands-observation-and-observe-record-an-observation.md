# The commands /observation and /observe record an observation. What do they become? — /note replaces them, selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> The commands /observation and /observe record an observation. What do they become?

The options:

- **/note replaces them** — /note and /item note record a note. /observation and /observe are removed
- **/note, keep old as aliases** — /note records a note. /observation and /observe stay as aliases of it
- **Something else — say what to change** — Describe the commands

The engineer's answer: **/note replaces them**
