# Work items will end Completed rather than Done. What happens to the /done command? — Retire /done, selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> Work items will end Completed rather than Done. What happens to the /done command?

The options:

- **Retire /done** — Remove /done. /complete ends a work item Completed, as it ends a feature.
- **Keep /done as alias** — Keep /done as another name for /complete, giving Completed.

The engineer's answer: **Retire /done**
