# For the improvement-kind item: does an improvement get its own command, /improvement, the way /idea creates an idea? — Add /improvement, selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> For the improvement-kind item: does an improvement get its own command, /improvement, the way /idea creates an idea?

The options:

- **Add /improvement** — /improvement creates an item tagged [improvement], as an alias of /item improvement, like /idea.
- **No command** — An improvement is created with /item improvement only.

The engineer's answer: **Add /improvement**
