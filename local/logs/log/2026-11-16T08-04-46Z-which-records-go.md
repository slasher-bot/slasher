# Which records go? — Every record naming a project, selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> Which records go?

The options:

- **The migration's records** — The migration record and conform-email.sh, the refresh-after-publish experiment record and its work session record, and the log entries about the migration or naming the projects. Other records that only mention a project have the name removed.
- **Every record naming a project** — Delete every record that names one of the projects or a folder of projects, including the three other progression records, two items, and today's fully private journal records.

The engineer's answer: **Every record naming a project**
