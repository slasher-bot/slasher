# For the improvement-kind item: what does the improvement kind say about a record? — A proposed change, selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> For the improvement-kind item: what does the improvement kind say about a record?

The options:

- **Only that it exists** — An improvement's subject already exists. The kind says nothing about whether the change is decided. It takes the states every item takes.
- **A proposed change** — An improvement's subject already exists, and the change is not yet decided. Concludes Realized when the change exists.
- **Work on what exists** — An improvement's subject already exists, and the change is decided. It is a task, and concludes Done.

The engineer's answer: **A proposed change**
