# Which word do you mean for the tagline's third clause, "Humanity reins" or "Humanity reigns"? — Humanity reigns., selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> Which word do you mean for the tagline's third clause, "Humanity reins" or "Humanity reigns"?

The options:

- **Humanity reins.** — Holds the reins: the person guides and restrains the agent. It is a figure of speech, and "rein" normally takes "in".
- **Humanity reigns.** — The person rules. It means about what "Humanity rules" meant, which you removed today.
- **Neither** — Drop this word and go back to the other verbs.

The engineer's answer: **Humanity reigns.**
