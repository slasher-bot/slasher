# What does configuring the records line set? — Discontinue the configure subcommand, selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> What does configuring the records line set?

The options:

- **Whether it prints** — Always, at the end of every reply that writes a record, as now; or never, so it prints only through /records show.
- **Whether and how** — Whether it prints, and its form: full absolute paths as now, or each record's name alone.

The engineer's answer: **Discontinue the configure subcommand**
