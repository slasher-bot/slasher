# You said the engineer can make a goal Active, or leave it Recorded. Does the same apply to an aspiration? — Goals and aspirations, selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> You said the engineer can make a goal Active, or leave it Recorded. Does the same apply to an aspiration?

The options:

- **Goals and aspirations** — start makes either one Active from Recorded, or it stays Recorded
- **Goals only** — An aspiration stays Recorded until it is Realized

The engineer's answer: **Goals and aspirations**
