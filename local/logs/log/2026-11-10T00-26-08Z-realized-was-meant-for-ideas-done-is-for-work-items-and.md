The question, put through the selection interface:

> Realized was meant for ideas, Done is for work items, and Completed is for implementations. Should the concluding states keep that pairing, or become one word?

The options:

- **Keep the pairing** — /complete makes an idea Realized, a work item Done, and a feature or experiment Completed. Three words, one command.
- **Completed for all** — Realized and Done are removed, and existing Realized and Done items become Completed. One word, one command.

The engineer's answer: **Keep the pairing**
