# What do a private session's 4 minutes count from? — Last prompt, selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> What do a private session's 4 minutes count from?

The options:

- **Start or last continue** — 4 minutes after the private session starts, or after the engineer last chose to continue it. Each Continue starts another 4 minutes.
- **Last prompt** — 4 minutes with no prompt from the engineer. An active private session never times out.

The engineer's answer: **Last prompt**
