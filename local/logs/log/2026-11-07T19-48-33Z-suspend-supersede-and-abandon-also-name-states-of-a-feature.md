# /suspend, /supersede, and /abandon also name states of a feature or experiment, whose commands are /feature suspend and the like. What should the bare forms act on? — Ask which, when unclear, selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> /suspend, /supersede, and /abandon also name states of a feature or experiment, whose commands are /feature suspend and the like. What should the bare forms act on?

The options:

- **Items only** — All nine are aliases of their /item forms and act only on items. /suspend never acts on a feature. The help says so.
- **Leave those three out** — Only /start, /realize, /done, /defer, /obsolete, and /reopen are made. The three that overlap stay /item-only.
- **Ask which, when unclear** — The three act on an item, or on a feature or experiment, and ask through the selection interface where the words do not say which. They are then more than aliases of /item.

The engineer's answer: **Ask which, when unclear**
