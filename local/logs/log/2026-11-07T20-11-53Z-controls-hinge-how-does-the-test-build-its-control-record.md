# Controls hinge: how does the test build its control record? — Record written inline, selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> Controls hinge: how does the test build its control record?

The options:

- **Record written inline** — The record is written out in full where the test needs it.
- **Control function** — A control function writes a record from its handle, state, and branch, for reuse by the later outcomes: more than one current, none current, and phases.

The engineer's answer: **Record written inline**
