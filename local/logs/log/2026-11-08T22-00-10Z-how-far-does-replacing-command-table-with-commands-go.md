# How far does replacing /command-table with /commands go? — The table's row only, selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> How far does replacing /command-table with /commands go?

The options:

- **The table's row only** — The full table's last row names /commands instead of /command-table. /command-table stays the command, and /commands and /? stay its aliases.
- **Rename the command** — /commands becomes the command everywhere: the table row, the rule, the help, and the skills. /command-table becomes an alias of /commands, beside /?.
- **Rename, drop the old name** — /commands becomes the command everywhere, and /command-table is removed. /? stays an alias of /commands.

The engineer's answer: **The table's row only**
