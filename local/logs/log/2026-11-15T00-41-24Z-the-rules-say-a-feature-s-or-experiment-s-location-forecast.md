**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> The rules say a feature's or experiment's location, forecast, and attended-or-unattended questions are asked "at the start", but they are asked when it is created, by `new` (Open) or by `start` where none is Open (Active). An Open progression's work "has not started" yet. How should the rules name that moment?

The options:

- **When it is created (Recommended)** — The rules say the questions are asked "when the progression is created", and the record's confirmations are "at its creation". "Start" then means only the work starting: Open to Active, or creation by start.
- **Keep "at the start"** — The wording stays. "The start" of a progression is read as its creation, though an Open progression's work has not started.

The engineer's answer: **When it is created (Recommended)**
