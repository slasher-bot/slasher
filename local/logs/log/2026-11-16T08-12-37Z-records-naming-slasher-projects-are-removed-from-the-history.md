# Every record naming one of the Slasher projects the migration from Waytide reached is removed, from master and from its history

**Tags:** [decision]

The engineer directed it on grounds of disclosure: the records named the projects, and were published. Thirty-eight records were deleted, with every earlier path each one had, and the tests of the email conversion script that went with them. In every other file and commit message in the history, each project's name was replaced with `some-project`, and the folder of projects with `~/src`. The history was rewritten and force-pushed to `origin`. Records that cite a deleted record by its name still do.
