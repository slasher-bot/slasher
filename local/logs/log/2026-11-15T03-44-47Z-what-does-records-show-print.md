# What does `/records show` print? — Last reply's records, selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> What does `/records show` print?

The options:

- **Last reply's records** — The records line of the previous reply: the full path of each record it wrote, as the Recorded in: list gives them.
- **This session's records** — Every record written in this session so far, as one Recorded in: list, oldest first.

The engineer's answer: **Last reply's records**
