# When the progression concludes, what happens to the item in the Progression state? — Follows the progression, selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> When the progression concludes, what happens to the item in the Progression state?

The options:

- **Follows the progression** — The item takes its kind's concluding state when the progression completes or is affirmed, and is asked about otherwise
- **Stays Progression** — The item keeps Progression for good, and the progression record carries the outcome
- **The engineer says** — The item stays Progression until the engineer concludes it

The engineer's answer: **Follows the progression**
