# Which outcome of current-implementation.sh does the loop take next? — Status line (Recommended), selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> Which outcome of current-implementation.sh does the loop take next?

The options:

- **Status line (Recommended)** — statusline.sh prints what current-implementation.sh prints, as its second line, and nothing where it prints nothing.
- **None current** — With no Active or Suspended record, it prints nothing.
- **Several current** — With more than one current record, it prints only the branch of the one most recently acted upon.
- **With a phase** — Where the implementation's phase is under way, it prints the phase's branch beside the implementation's.

The engineer's answer: **Status line (Recommended)**
