# What does the "Rename consuming projects" work cover? — The term everywhere, selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> What does the "Rename consuming projects" work cover?

The options:

- **The registry only** — The file becomes index.toml, and read-consuming-projects.sh, its tests, and the text describing the registry are renamed. "Consuming project" stays the term for a project that installs Slasher.
- **The term everywhere** — The file becomes index.toml, and "consuming project" is replaced in every rule, README, script, and test with a new term, settled next. Historical records keep the old word.

The engineer's answer: **The term everywhere**
