# `refresh` appears in three commands: `/packages refresh`, `/packages <package> refresh`, and `/package refresh`. Where does `update` replace it? — Everywhere, selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> `refresh` appears in three commands: `/packages refresh`, `/packages <package> refresh`, and `/package refresh`. Where does `update` replace it?

The options:

- **In /packages only** — `/packages update` and `/packages <package> update`. `/package refresh` keeps its name.
- **Everywhere** — `/packages update`, `/packages <package> update`, and `/package update`. No command is named `refresh` any more.
- **Something else — say what to change** — Some other set of commands. Say which.

The engineer's answer: **Everywhere**
