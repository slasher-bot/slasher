# What does a correction item record? It must differ from a mistake (an act that went wrong), a problem (something wrong, no solution named), and an improvement (a change to something that already exists). — A fix to something wrong, selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> What does a correction item record? It must differ from a mistake (an act that went wrong), a problem (something wrong, no solution named), and an improvement (a change to something that already exists).

The options:

- **A fix to something wrong** — A proposed change that puts right something existing that is wrong, such as a false statement in a rule. Concludes Realized when the fix exists. An improvement then covers changes to things that are not wrong.
- **The engineer corrected the agent** — What the agent did or said, and what the engineer corrected it to, recorded so the correction is not lost
- **A fix already made** — A record, written after the fact, of something that was wrong and was put right

The engineer's answer: **A fix to something wrong**
