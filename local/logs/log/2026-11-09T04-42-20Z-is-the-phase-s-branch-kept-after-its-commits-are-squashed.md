# Is the phase's branch kept after its commits are squashed? — Kept until the implementation concludes, selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> Is the phase's branch kept after its commits are squashed?

The options:

- **Deleted on confirmation (Recommended)** — After the squash, the phase's branch is deleted, on one confirmation, as an implementation's branch is.
- **Kept until the implementation concludes** — Kept, so its commits can be read, and deleted with the implementation's branch.
- **Kept** — Never deleted.

The engineer's answer: **Kept until the implementation concludes**
