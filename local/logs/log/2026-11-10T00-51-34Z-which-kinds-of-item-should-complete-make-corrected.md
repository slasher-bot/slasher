# Which kinds of item should /complete make Corrected? — Mistake and correction, selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> Which kinds of item should /complete make Corrected?

The options:

- **Problem and mistake** — /complete makes a problem or a mistake Corrected. A correction stays a work item and ends Done.
- **Mistake and correction** — /complete makes a mistake or a correction Corrected. A correction no longer ends Done, and a problem's end is still unsettled.
- **All three** — /complete makes a problem, a mistake, or a correction Corrected.

The engineer's answer: **Mistake and correction**
