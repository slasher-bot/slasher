# Which commands does the feature for "Progressions can be created and started directly" add? — Progression, aliased, selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> Which commands does the feature for "Progressions can be created and started directly" add?

The options:

- **As written** — /evolution and /evolutions are the commands, and /implementation and /implementations are their aliases. A progression is started with /evolution.
- **Progression, aliased** — /progression and /progressions are the commands, and /implementation and /implementations are their aliases.
- **Progression, both aliases** — /progression and /progressions are the commands, and /implementation, /implementations, /evolution, and /evolutions are all aliases.

The engineer's answer: **Progression, aliased**
