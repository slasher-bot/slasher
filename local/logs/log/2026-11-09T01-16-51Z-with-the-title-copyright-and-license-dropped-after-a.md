# With the title, copyright, and license dropped after a compaction, what should show in their place? — A one-line notice (Recommended), selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> With the title, copyright, and license dropped after a compaction, what should show in their place?

The options:

- **A one-line notice (Recommended)** — One line, such as: Context compacted. Slasher is reloading its rules.
- **The installed-packages notice** — Only the existing line: Slasher installed at system/ — 7 packages: … with no load line.
- **Nothing** — Show nothing after a compaction. The agent still reloads the rules.

The engineer's answer: **A one-line notice (Recommended)**
