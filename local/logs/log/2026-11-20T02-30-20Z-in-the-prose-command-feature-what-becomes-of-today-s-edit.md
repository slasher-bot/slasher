# In the /prose command feature, what becomes of today's /edit and /review once /prose edit and /prose suggest exist? — Keep as short forms, selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> In the /prose command feature, what becomes of today's /edit and /review once /prose edit and /prose suggest exist?

The options:

- **Remove both** — /edit and /review are removed. /prose edit and /prose suggest are the only commands for those acts.
- **Keep as short forms** — /edit stays as a short form of /prose edit, and /review as a short form of /prose suggest.

The engineer's answer: **Keep as short forms**
