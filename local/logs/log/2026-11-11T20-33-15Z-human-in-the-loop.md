# Human in the loop — Attended (Recommended, Confidence: 85%), selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> Human in the loop
>
> - Agent's confidence: 60% (how sure the agent is about working alone.)
> - Risk of redo: 40% (how likely it is that you will have to redo work the agent does.)
> - It'll work: 80% (how sure the agent is that what it builds on its own does what it says.)
> - Your approval: 60% (how sure the agent is that it does what you would do: naming, shaping, and deciding things as you would.)
>
> When the agent's confidence is 70% or lower, intervention is indicated.

The options:

- **Attended (Recommended, Confidence: 85%)** — You decide. The agent automates.
- **Unattended** — The agent makes the decisions and generates through if you accept the plan it'll propose.

The engineer's answer: **Attended (Recommended, Confidence: 85%)**
