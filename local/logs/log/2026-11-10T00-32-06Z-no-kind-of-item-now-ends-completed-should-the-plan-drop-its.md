# No kind of item now ends Completed. Should the plan drop its decision that items gain the Completed state? — Keep it, selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> No kind of item now ends Completed. Should the plan drop its decision that items gain the Completed state?

The options:

- **Drop it** — Items don't gain Completed. Only features and experiments end Completed, and /complete on an item gives Realized or Done.
- **Keep it** — Items keep Completed for a kind not yet named, such as a problem or a mistake.

The engineer's answer: **Keep it**
