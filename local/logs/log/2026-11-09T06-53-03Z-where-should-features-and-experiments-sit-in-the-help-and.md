# Where should /features and /experiments sit in the help and the command table? Either way, each lists the current records of its kind (Active or Suspended), takes an optional number to limit the list to the most recent, and ends with counts of the other states, as /items does. — In their own domains (Recommended), selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> Where should /features and /experiments sit in the help and the command table? Either way, each lists the current records of its kind (Active or Suspended), takes an optional number to limit the list to the most recent, and ends with counts of the other states, as /items does.

The options:

- **In their own domains (Recommended)** — /features goes in the FEATURES help beside /feature, and /experiments in the EXPERIMENTS help beside /experiment. Each gets a command-table row below its domain's row.
- **In PROCESS, beside /items** — Both go in the PROCESS help next to /items. Each gets a command-table row below the /items row.

The engineer's answer: **In their own domains (Recommended)**
