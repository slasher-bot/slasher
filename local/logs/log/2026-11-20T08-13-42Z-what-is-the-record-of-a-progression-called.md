# What is the record of a progression called? — Progression record, selected by the engineer

**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> What is the record of a progression called?

The options:

- **Progression record** — The work is a progression, and its file is a progression record, as an implementation record is now
- **Progression** — One word for both the work and its file

The engineer's answer: **Progression record**
