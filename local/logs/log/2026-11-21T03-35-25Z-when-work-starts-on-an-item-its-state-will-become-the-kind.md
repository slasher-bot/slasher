**Tags:** [decision]

The question, put through the selection interface:

> When work starts on an item, its state will become the kind of that work: Implementation, Feature, Experiment, or Content. Today such an item is hidden from /' (/current), because its work is listed there under Progressions, and shown in /i (/items current). Should an item in one of the new states be treated the same way?

The options:

- **Same as today** — Hidden from /', since its work is listed under Progressions. Shown in /i.
- **Shown in both** — /' and /i both list the item, beside its work.
- **Hidden from both** — Neither list shows the item. Only its work is listed.

The engineer's answer: **Same as today**
