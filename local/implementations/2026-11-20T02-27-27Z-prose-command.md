## Plan, as suggested on 2026-11-19

1. Make PROSE the command domain, in place of REVIEW. `/prose help` prints its help.
2. `/prose edit`, from today's `/edit`. Sentences in a paragraph are numbered s1, s2. Each suggestion takes `accept`, `reject`, or `ignore`.
3. `/prose suggest`, from today's `/review`.
4. `/prose accept` and `/prose reject`, from `/review accept` and `/review reject`, with `/accept` and `/reject` as short forms.
5. `/prose critique`, an overall critical review against the `prose` package's criteria.
6. `/print-and-review` and `/pr` are removed.
7. What becomes of `/edit` and `/review` is the engineer's decision, and is not yet made.
8. The README and the help are brought up to date, the packages compiled, and the automated suite run.
